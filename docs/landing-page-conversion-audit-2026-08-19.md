# Neon Landing Page Conversion Audit — 2026-08-19

## Purpose

This audit reviews the public Neon landing page against the product-led clarity and guided self-service experience expected from leading agent platforms. It adapts useful interaction principles without copying third-party visual assets, copy, or brand identity.

## Current strengths

| Area | What is already working |
|---|---|
| Product story | The page establishes a clear three-step path: connect channels, train the agent, and launch. |
| Primary action | The “build your first agent free” action is visible above the fold and routes into the authenticated onboarding path. |
| Regional positioning | The page is Arabic-first and highlights Arabic/English support, WhatsApp, and a no-card trial. |
| Visual foundation | The deep-ink, cyan, and lime system offers a differentiated Neon starting point. |

## Conversion gaps to address

| Priority | Observed gap | Planned improvement |
|---|---|---|
| High | The product simulation is smaller and less decisive than the headline, so the service is not immediately tangible. | Build a larger, layered product proof panel that shows channel status, a live request, AI response, human handoff, and knowledge grounding. |
| High | Navigation and several labels are English-first despite the Arabic target customer. | Make the visible navigation and value framing Arabic-first while preserving concise English support labels where useful. |
| High | Trust signals are limited to generic claims. | Add truthful operating signals: 14-day trial, encrypted tenant separation, approval-aware WhatsApp onboarding, and local payment readiness. Never use fabricated customers, logos, testimonials, or ratings. |
| Medium | Repeated rounded card grids make all sections feel visually equivalent. | Use a stronger sequence: product proof, outcome bands, guided steps, vertical use cases, then conversion. |
| Medium | The hero states features but does not clearly establish a business outcome. | Lead with the customer outcome: answer customers, qualify inquiries, and hand difficult conversations to the team. |

## Design decision

The refinement keeps **Neon’s dark neon identity**, but uses the product interface as the dominant visual proof. It will use an Arabic-first content rhythm, stronger spacing hierarchy, one conversion intent, and accessible contrast. Any WhatsApp claim will remain approval-aware while Meta Access Verification is under review.
