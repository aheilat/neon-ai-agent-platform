# Arabic-Market Differentiation Research

## Competitive baseline

Intercom states that Fin supports customer questions in 45 languages and exposes language-filtered metrics including resolution, deflection, answer, involvement, and unresolved-question reporting. This makes language-aware quality measurement a baseline capability rather than a future enhancement for Neon.[1]

## Neon opportunity

Neon can differentiate in the Arabic and Gulf market by measuring Arabic quality at a finer operational level: Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf dialect, Arabizi, Arabic-English mixed messages, and channel-specific WhatsApp outcomes. The next Neon quality dashboard should therefore show unresolved intents, escalation reasons, missing knowledge, and resolution performance per agent, channel, and detected language style.
