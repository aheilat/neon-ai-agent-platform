# Neon AI — Controlled Gulf Launch Kit

**Launch URL:** https://agent.neonadai.com  
**Prepared:** 25 August 2026  
**Launch position:** Arabic/Gulf-first AI customer-response platform for businesses that want to answer routine inquiries, capture qualified requests, and hand complex cases to people.

## 1. The honest offer to sell now

> **Neon AI turns a company’s website and approved business knowledge into an Arabic-first customer agent.** It helps businesses respond clearly to common questions, qualify enquiries, and escalate the important conversations to the right person.

The public site, registration flow, isolated workspace, and direct Claude response path are live at the launch URL. The first commercial offer should be a **managed founding-customer pilot**, rather than a promise that every channel or custom integration is ready for every business on day one.

| Sell now | State it carefully | Do not promise yet |
|---|---|---|
| Website-based customer agent | The business supplies its public website, FAQs, pricing, and policies; Neon prepares the agent for review. | That the agent knows unpublished or changing information without a supplied source. |
| Arabic and English conversation experience | The agent is designed for Arabic-first conversations and can support bilingual customer communication. | A perfect local dialect in every country without testing it with the customer. |
| Enquiry qualification and human escalation | The agent can collect the customer’s stated need and hand off cases that require human judgment. | That it replaces sales, clinical, legal, or complaint-resolution judgment. |
| A guided first launch | Neon can help the founding customer prepare knowledge, test conversations, and improve the agent before wider use. | A fully self-service WhatsApp activation for all customers today. |

**Channel boundary:** present WhatsApp as a planned official channel that opens only after the relevant Meta/WhatsApp eligibility and customer configuration are complete. Do not claim that a customer’s own WhatsApp number is already connected unless that customer sees its confirmed connection status.

## 2. First customer profile

Start with businesses where many enquiries repeat, response speed matters, and the team already spends time answering website or WhatsApp messages.

| Priority | Best early sectors | Typical repeated questions | Pilot outcome to demonstrate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local services, vehicle rental, home services, marketing agencies | Availability, service scope, location, timing, quote request | A qualified enquiry with the customer’s need, date, location, and contact preference. |
| 2 | Real estate and property services | Listing details, area, budget, viewing availability | A structured viewing or callback request passed to a human. |
| 3 | E-commerce and catalog businesses | Product information, delivery, returns, order questions | Accurate answers from approved product and policy knowledge. |
| Later | Healthcare, finance, legal, or other sensitive sectors | High-stakes or regulated questions | A tightly constrained enquiry and booking assistant, with clear human escalation—not advice. |

This choice is practical: regional guidance consistently highlights Arabic language quality, business-specific knowledge, and human escalation as central deployment concerns, while WhatsApp remains an important customer-contact channel in the Gulf.[1] [2] [3]

## 3. Founding-customer pilot offer

Offer a limited, hands-on pilot to **five businesses only**. This makes the launch credible and gives Neon a manageable learning loop.

| Pilot element | What the customer receives | What Neon asks for |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery call | A short session to map common customer questions and the desired handoff process. | One decision-maker and one customer-facing team member. |
| Knowledge onboarding | A structured review of the public website, FAQs, pricing, service descriptions, and approved documents. | Permission to use the supplied material and confirmation that it is current. |
| Agent configuration | An Arabic-first tone, defined goals, and clear boundaries for when to escalate. | The business approves the opening message and escalation wording. |
| Test period | A controlled test with real internal scenarios before customer-facing promotion. | Feedback on wrong, missing, or overly confident answers. |
| Launch review | A summary of enquiry quality, unanswered questions, and recommended improvements. | A decision to continue, adjust, or pause after the pilot. |

Do not publish a heavily discounted “forever” price. Instead, describe this as a **founding-customer pilot** and agree the commercial price after the discovery call, based on the number of agents, knowledge scope, support level, and live channels required.

## 6. Seven-day no-spam launch plan

| Day | Action | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish the launch URL in the company bio, LinkedIn page, and founder profile. | The URL, login, and contact email work from a normal browser. |
| 2 | Build a list of 30 warm, relevant Gulf businesses in one priority sector. | Every contact has a concrete reason to care, not just a phone number. |
| 3 | Send the short introduction to 10 warm contacts individually. | At least two replies or one demo request. |
| 4 | Publish one founder post showing the problem: missed enquiries and repetitive questions. | Interested people ask what Neon does or request a demo. |
| 5 | Run two discovery calls and record the top 10 repeated customer questions. | Clear knowledge sources and a defined handoff path. |
| 6 | Configure one controlled pilot and test it with Arabic and English questions. | The customer approves the scope and identifies gaps before public use. |
| 7 | Publish a lessons-learned post without naming customers or inventing results. | More qualified prospects enter the pilot list. |

## 7. Content themes for the first month

| Theme | Message | Suggested format |
|---|---|---|
| Lost enquiries | “Your team should not answer the same question all day.” | Short Arabic video or carousel. |
| Knowledge, not generic chat | “The agent answers from your approved services, pricing, and policies.” | Screen recording of the setup journey. |
| Human control | “AI handles repeatable questions; people make the sensitive decisions.” | Founder post with an escalation example. |
| Gulf-first experience | “Arabic conversation quality, bilingual context, and clear handoff matter.” | Arabic/English comparison post. |
| Pilot invitation | “We are inviting five founding businesses to test a guided customer agent.” | Direct call-to-action post. |

Avoid invented customer testimonials, invented savings figures, fabricated ratings, or unverified claims about WhatsApp approval. If a result comes from a pilot, obtain the customer’s written approval before using its name or figures.

## 8. Sales qualification checklist

Ask these questions in the first call. If the business cannot answer them, it is not ready for a customer-facing pilot yet.

1. Which three customer questions repeat most often?
2. What source should the agent treat as approved: website, price sheet, catalog, FAQ, or policy document?
3. What must the agent never answer or decide on its own?
4. When should the conversation move to a human, and who receives it?
5. Which language and tone do customers use most often?
6. What outcome matters first: fewer repetitive questions, more qualified leads, faster first response, or better booking requests?
7. Which channel is genuinely ready now: website, email, or an officially configured WhatsApp account?

## 9. Launch measurement

Track only signals that support a real sales decision.

| Funnel stage | Metric | First 30-day target |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Relevant landing-page visits and post replies | Establish a baseline, then improve the channel with the highest-quality enquiries. |
| Interest | Demo requests | 5–10 qualified requests. |
| Qualification | Discovery calls completed | 5 calls with businesses that have a real knowledge source and response workflow. |
| Activation | Pilots started | 2–3 controlled pilots. |
| Product learning | Unanswered or escalated question categories | A weekly list used to improve knowledge and instructions. |

## 10. Immediate founder checklist

The domain is ready. Before paying for ads or broadly promoting the service, keep the launch controlled:

1. Use **https://agent.neonadai.com** as the one public launch URL.
2. Offer a guided founding-customer pilot, not unrestricted production promises.
3. Target one vertical first—local services, property, or e-commerce.
4. Ask every pilot for approved knowledge and a named human escalation owner.
5. Promote WhatsApp only as an official channel when each customer’s account is actually configured and confirmed.
